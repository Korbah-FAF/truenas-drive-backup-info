TrueNAS Drive Backup

Terms of use

An owner-operated personal backup integration.

Intended use

TrueNAS Drive Backup is intended for its owner's personal use with their own Google account and private server. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept public registrations or payments.

The owner selects the files to back up, authorises Google Drive access and controls backup schedules, retention and recovery. Only files the owner is entitled to store should be included.

Operation and recovery

Successful recovery depends on retaining the repository encryption password, maintaining a working cloud connection, preserving repository contents and testing restores. The owner is responsible for monitoring backup failures and available storage.

This integration uses independently maintained software and Google's storage service. Their own licences, policies and service terms continue to apply.

Privacy and support

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. Questions can be directed to the support email shown on the application's Google consent screen.
